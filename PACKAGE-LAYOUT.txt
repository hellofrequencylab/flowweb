Dependency rule (lint-enforced):
  contracts depends on nothing.
  eventlog depends on contracts.
  ledger depends on contracts and eventlog.
  policy depends on contracts, eventlog and ledger.
  adapters depends on contracts only (vendor names live here and nowhere else).
  daemon depends on all of the above (it is the composition root).
  cli talks to the daemon over the generated client and may read the database directly only for read-only commands and for `fw kill`.
  web depends on the generated client and its own ui-kit only; it never imports a kernel package.
  Only daemon/src/creds touches secret material.
